Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Jabrow and tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the subscription screen, signed in to the same Apple Account that bought it. This restores access, not training progress.

How do I cancel?

In Jabrow: Settings → Manage Subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Jabrow → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How does the free trial work?

If the yearly plan shows a free trial, the App shows how long it lasts and what is charged afterwards before you start. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends if you do not want to be charged.

I want a refund.

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If your subscription status looks wrong in the App, email us and we will help.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Jabrow has no account and no cloud sync: your progress, XP, rank and settings are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them, and they do not move to another phone.

I can’t hear the coach.

  • Turn the volume up with the side buttons. Jabrow plays the coach even when the ringer is on silent.
  • If a Bluetooth speaker, car or earbuds are connected, the sound plays there. Pick the output in Control Center.
  • A phone call pauses the session; other audio apps and navigation prompts can interrupt it too. Close them and resume.
  • Background music can be turned off in Settings; the coach voice stays on.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Coach voices and music are built into the app. An internet connection is only needed to buy, restore or check a subscription.

Is it safe for me?

Jabrow is fitness content, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting, stop if you feel pain or dizziness, and train alone in a clear space. See the Terms of Use.

What data does Jabrow collect?

No account is needed and your training data stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
