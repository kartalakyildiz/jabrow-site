Privacy Policy

Jabrow: Muay Thai & Boxing · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Jabrow iPhone app (the “App”) handles, why, and what choices you have. The App is developed by Kartal Akyildiz and published on the App Store by Batuhan Soyarslan, both based in Turkey (“we”, “us”). In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and your training data stays on your iPhone.

1. Summary

  • No account, login or cloud sync. We do not run a server that stores your training data.
  • Your setup answers, training progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App does not use your camera, microphone, location services, contacts, photos or HealthKit.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites, and we never sell your data.
  • Subscriptions are paid through Apple and verified by RevenueCat.
  • Anonymous usage analytics (PostHog) help us see which parts of the App work and where people get stuck. They are not linked to your identity.

2. Information stored on your device

The App keeps the following in a local database on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Setup answers: the fighter look you picked, your training goal, Muay Thai experience, lead hand (stance), equipment, training days, session length, what usually breaks your routine, and your chosen coach.
  • Height and weight: asked during setup only to show a weight class on screen. They are not used for health purposes and never leave your device.
  • Training progress: completed sessions and rounds, experience points (XP), rank, rank exam results and streaks.
  • Settings: for example whether music is on, which tips you have already seen, and whether the App has already shown Apple’s rating dialog.

The App does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

3. Purchases and RevenueCat

Jabrow offers auto-renewable subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your card details or your Apple Account credentials.

To check whether your subscription is active, the App uses RevenueCat, which processes purchase information on our behalf. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created for your installation, purchase and receipt information from Apple (such as which plan you bought and whether it is active) and basic technical information (device type, iOS version, country). It uses this only to validate purchases. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

“Restore Purchases” asks Apple and RevenueCat to confirm your subscription again. It restores access, not training progress.

4. Anonymous usage analytics (PostHog)

The App sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, an analytics service, so we can improve the App. Events are tied to a random ID created for your installation, not to your name, Apple Account or any other identity, and we do not combine them with data from other companies.

  • What is sent: which screens are opened, which setup steps are reached, whether the demo was played, when the subscription screen is shown and which plan is picked, whether a purchase or restore succeeded or failed, and when a training session starts, finishes or is ended early (with its length, rounds, XP, the coach and whether it was a rank exam).
  • Device information: device model, iOS version and app version.
  • Approximate location: PostHog receives your IP address when the App connects and may use it to estimate a country and city. We do not collect precise location.
  • Never sent: your setup answers, height, weight, goal or any text you could type.

See the PostHog Privacy Policy.

5. Audio and device features

Coach voices, sound effects and music are built into the App. The App does not record sound. During a session it keeps the screen awake and keeps the coach talking when the screen is locked.

So that a session can pause when you get a phone call, the App checks whether a call is in progress. It does not read who is calling or any other call details, and this check never leaves your device.

The App may show Apple’s standard rating dialog once; ratings are handled by Apple and we receive nothing about you from it.

6. Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Deleting the App permanently removes your setup answers, progress and settings; we cannot recover them because we have no copy. You can also clear your progress inside the App (Settings → Reset progress).

Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies and legal obligations. Anonymous analytics events are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the App. To ask about your data or request deletion of a purchase record, email jabrow@bkverse.com. Because the App has no account, we may need extra information (for example an Apple order receipt) to find a record.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The exercises are meant for adults, and for teenagers only with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian.

8. International users

We are based in Turkey. The providers named above (Apple, RevenueCat, PostHog) may process data in other countries, including the United States. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the United Kingdom or California) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Contact us to use them.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the App’s private storage and protected by iOS. Connections to RevenueCat and PostHog are encrypted. We keep the data we handle to a minimum.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date shows the current version.
